WE'VE REACHED A POINT OF NO RETURN

There is a forest still burning behind your ribs
a humble stiffened crackle like knuckles in winter rush
the glaciers keep their old shapes in photographs
in heat they loosen like woodrot you swear you feel them calving
somewhere a limb you never had cleaving the river remembers its wide shoulders
now it moves through a narrow death bed like a barbered leaf
there used to be bees in the forget me nots their soft engines hovering
at the elbow of every afternoon
now the silence
shadows pressing over your shoulder on the forest floor
how bodies make light
complicated you press your hand
to the stump of the season and wait
for green
to answer
